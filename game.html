Welcome to Gem Forge!

Match 3 or more identical gems by swapping adjacent ones. Earn gems and spend them to place pixels on the shared canvas.

🔀

How to Swap

Click a gem to select it, then click an adjacent gem to swap them. If the swap creates a match of 3+, the gems break and you score points!

🔥

Combos & Specials

Chain reactions trigger combos for multiplied scores. Match 4+ gems to create special gems like Rockets, Bombs, and Color Blasts!

🚀

Special Gems

4-match: Rocket — clears entire row or column
5-match: Bomb — destroys 3×3 area
L/T-shape: Color Blast — removes all gems of that color
6+-match: Nova — devastating 5×5 blast

💎

Boosters

Spend gems on powerful boosters: Hammer destroys a single gem, Shuffle rearranges the board, +5 Moves gives extra turns, and more!

🎨

Earn & Create

Score points to earn gems and XP. Use gems on the Canvas page to place pixels on the shared 200×200 grid. Create art together!
